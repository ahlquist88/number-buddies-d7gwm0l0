Privacy

Number Buddies is a free maths and spelling app made by a parent. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Without an account

Everything stays on the device you use: the child's first name, the buddy they chose, their school year, stars, unfinished rounds, the questions they found tricky, word lists you add, and the grown-up PIN. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing the browser's data removes it.

Photos of word lists

When you take a photo of a spelling list, the words are read on your own device. The photo is never uploaded or stored. The app downloads the free word-reading program (Tesseract) and its language file the first time; after that, only the words you keep are saved, as part of the word list.

With a free family account

If a grown-up signs in, the same information is also stored in the family's own record in Google Firebase, so it can be used on more than one device. The account holds the grown-up's email address (or Google sign-in) and the family record. Children never need an email address or a password.

  • Only the signed-in grown-up can read or change their family record.
  • The information is used only to run the app. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • You can delete the account and the online record at any time under For grown-ups → Account. Progress on your device is kept unless you clear it.

Voices

The teachers speak with the voices built into your device. The text is turned into speech by the device itself.
