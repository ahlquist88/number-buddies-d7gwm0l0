Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Number Buddies is a free maths and spelling app for children. It is a hobby project made by a parent. It has no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no cookies. This page explains what information the app uses, where it is kept and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

The person responsible for your information (the “data controller”) is the parent who made Number Buddies, a private individual. You can reach me at ahlquist2215@gmail.com.

2. Using the app without an account

You can use Number Buddies without an account. Everything is then saved only in the browser on your own device:

  • each child’s first name (or nickname), the buddy they chose, their school year and app language
  • stars, finished and unfinished rounds, the results of the starting quiz, and the questions they found tricky
  • the family’s word lists
  • the grown-up PIN, saved in a scrambled form (a “hash”), not as the PIN itself
  • the chosen reading voice for each language, saved for this device only

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. I cannot see it. It stays until you delete it in the app (under For grown-ups) or clear the browser’s data for this site.

3. Using the app with a free family account

A grown-up can choose to create a free family account to keep progress safe and use it on more than one device. Only a grown-up can create an account. Children never need an email address or a password.

With an account, the following is also stored online:

  • Sign-in details: the grown-up’s email address and password, or their Google sign-in. Passwords are handled by Google’s sign-in service; I never see them. If you sign in with Google, Google also passes on your name and profile picture link. The app only shows the email address. While you are signed in, the browser keeps a sign-in key so you stay signed in.
  • The family record: the same information as in section 2 (children’s first names, buddies, school years, progress, word lists and the PIN hash). The reading voice is not included.

Only the signed-in grown-up can read or change their family record. This is enforced by the database’s security rules. I do not look at family records, except if you ask me to help with a problem.

4. Photos of word lists

When you take or choose a photo of a spelling list, the words are read on your own device. The photo is never uploaded, sent or stored. Only the words you keep are saved, as part of the word list. To read photos, the app first downloads a free word-reading program (Tesseract) and a language file from jsDelivr (see section 6).

5. Why the information is used, and the legal basis

The information is used only to run the app: to show each child’s progress, pick the right questions, keep word lists and, with an account, keep everything in step across devices. It is never sold, shared for marketing or used for advertising. No decisions are made about anyone by automated means.

  • Family account: the legal basis is consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). The grown-up gives consent when they create the account, for themselves and for the children they add. Information about children is only ever added by a parent or another grown-up with parental responsibility. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the account (see section 8).
  • Loading the website: when the app opens, the services in section 6 receive your device’s internet address, because that is how the internet delivers a page. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the app safely and working (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Saving on the device: the app saves only what is strictly needed for the features you use, so it does not ask for cookie consent.

6. Outside services

Number Buddies uses these services. Each sees your device’s internet address when the app contacts it.

Google Firebase acts as a processor for me under Google’s data processing terms. The family records are stored in Google’s data centres in the EU. Google’s sign-in service, GitHub and jsDelivr may also handle data outside the EU, including in the USA. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

The letter styles (fonts) are stored on the website itself, so no font service is contacted. The voices are built into your device, and the device turns the text into speech itself.

7. How long information is kept

  • On the device: until you delete it in the app or clear the browser’s data.
  • Family account: until you delete it. If you ask me by email, I will delete it within 30 days.
  • After deletion: the family record is removed from the live database straight away. Google may keep copies in its own backups for a limited time, as set out in its terms, before they are deleted for good.
  • Website visits: GitHub, Google and jsDelivr keep their own short-term technical records under their own privacy policies. I have no access to them.

8. Deleting your data in the app

  • To remove a child: For grown-ups → Children → Remove.
  • To clear a child’s stars and saved rounds: For grown-ups → Settings → Reset progress.
  • To delete the account and everything stored online: For grown-ups → Account → Delete the account and online data. Progress on the device is kept until you clear it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • see the information held about you and your children (access)
  • have it corrected (correction)
  • have it deleted (erasure)
  • limit or object to its use
  • receive it in a common format (portability)
  • withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect what was done before

Most of this you can do yourself in the app. Otherwise, email ahlquist2215@gmail.com and I will reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark this is Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, www.datatilsynet.dk. You can also complain to the authority in the country where you live.

10. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Online family records can only be read by the grown-up who is signed in. The grown-up PIN is there to stop children changing settings by accident. It is not a password, so please don’t use a code you use anywhere else.

11. Changes to this page

If the way the app uses information changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
